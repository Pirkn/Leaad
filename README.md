# Leaad 🚀  
**AI-Powered Reddit Lead Generation**

Leaad helps you **get your product in front of the right people without guessing**.  
From product analysis to lead discovery and AI-generated posts, we handle the heavy lifting so you can focus on building.

## ✨ Features

- **Real-Time Lead Discovery**  
  - Scan Reddit 24/7 for discussions where your product fits.  
  - Intent-based targeting with sentiment analysis.  
  - Context-aware AI response generation.  

- **Smart Post Generation**  
  - AI-crafted posts and comments that sound native.  
  - Pain-point focused copy designed to drive conversions.  
  - You keep full control—nothing gets posted without you.  

- **Viral Templates**  
  - Plug-and-play Reddit-friendly formats based on proven hits.  
  - Optimized structure for engagement and shareability.  

- **Compliance & Safety**  
  - Stay Reddit TOS-friendly (no auto-posting, no spam).  
  - Guidance to avoid bans and build authentic credibility.  
  - Practical tips for sustainable community growth.  

- **Karma Builder**  
  - Build a credible presence on Reddit.  
  - Avoid low-effort bans and gain visibility in your niche.

## 🛠 How It Works

1. **Enter Your Product**  
   Provide your product URL—our AI analyzes your target audience, problems solved, and positioning.  

2. **We Find Your Leads**  
   Our system scans Reddit to surface buying signals, pain points, and relevant discussions.  

3. **Interact & Convert**  
   Use AI-generated, personalized responses to engage with leads and turn them into customers.
